# E3g draft: atomic reminder claim + durable notification outbox

## Invariants and persistence
Extend **only** FocusReminderCollectionStore's private-file snapshot to envelope version 2. Read version 1 with an empty outbox; write version 2 after a mutation. Reject future versions, version 1 carrying an outbox field, duplicate dispatch IDs or ticket IDs, malformed dates, outbox records for disabled/imported/deleted reminder IDs, and over-capacity files. Version 2 is **not** readable by previous binaries: make a backup and explicitly approve rollback/migration before any shipping upgrade. The existing per-reminder FocusReminderStore is not silently migrated and retains its prior claim-without-outbox semantics.

For a native enabled reminder, consuming a due ordinary ticket or due Snooze ticket and appending its delivery intent must be one actor-owned, same-file atomic replacement. Only return a claim and enqueued record after a successful write. The intent includes independent idempotency UUID, reminder UUID, original ticket UUID, kind, absolute deadline and claim time, but no title, secrets or OS-notification metadata. The sender may retry by the stable outbox UUID; record existence is neither OS registration nor user delivery proof. Outbox is bounded to 128 unacknowledged intents, independent of the 64 reminder records; fail closed with outboxFull **before** consuming a ticket when full. No eviction of unacknowledged intents. Repeated claims are rejected by state ticket fences.

After the notification adapter explicitly accepts an intent, acknowledge its exact outbox UUID with an atomic write. Unknown/replayed acknowledgement is staleOutbox; a write failure leaves the original record intact for an idempotent retry. Without an OS adapter this contract offers durable intent only: it cannot claim at-least-once *delivery*, exactly-once presentation, delivery receipts or a working notification UI.

## Cancellation and lifecycle
Disable, delete and non-no-op rule replacement revoke that reminder's unacknowledged intents in the same transaction. Rename keeps them. Explicit Snooze removes any unacknowledged original ordinary intent. A newer ordinary claim removes older pending Snooze intents for that reminder. This is the **local outbox** cancellation policy only; future OS-registration cancellation is a separate side effect and must have its own reconciliation semantics.

At app restart, load and validate the durable queue before any dispatch. Due-candidate queries (#449) remain read-only and do not claim. Recheck ticket IDs and calendars through the authoritative store. A future coordinator must define missed deadlines, permission denied, duplicated system notification IDs, retries/backoff, concurrent processes, acknowledgement semantics, crashes between OS acceptance and ack, and OS cancel failures. Atomic replacement alone does not guarantee fsync/power-loss durability. The store assumes a single process/actor owner. No background notification registration, migration, UI, rights/ADR, Runner archival or main-promotion change in this slice.
